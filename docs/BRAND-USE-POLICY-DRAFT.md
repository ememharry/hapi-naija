# Hapi Naija brand-use policy — draft for owner sign-off

**Proposed policy owner:** Emem Harry Etukudo

This policy concerns use of the Hapi Naija name, logo and visual identity; it does not restrict rights that would be granted under a future Apache License 2.0 release of software.

## Generally permitted
- Truthful references to Hapi Naija in reporting, research, presentations, and project documentation.
- Linking to the official repository.
- Attribution such as “based on Hapi Naija software” without implying endorsement.

## Written permission required
- Describing an independent fork or deployment as an official Hapi Naija service.
- Using Hapi Naija branding as the primary identity of another app or organisation.
- Implying project endorsement in a fundraiser, sponsored event, campaign, or commercial promotion.
- Creating misleadingly similar project identities.

Independent deployments should identify their own operators and publish their own data-handling and privacy disclosures. No project may falsely claim Hapi Naija is owned by government or that it receives official complaints.

**Status:** Draft. No trademark registration is asserted. Approval and legal review pending.
